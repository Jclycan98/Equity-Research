## Executive Summary & Macro Thesis

Strategy #1 exploits the structural asymmetry between **scalable network economy winners with expanding operating leverage** versus **businesses suffering terminal disruption from foundational AI models and value-destroying equity dilution**:

1. **Buy Side (LONG - 100% Gross / 25% each):** Dominant software, cloud, and physical AI infrastructure platforms (`AMZN`, `ANET`, `SPOT`, `UBER`) that have surpassed heavy investment phases and now convert incremental revenue into exponential Free Cash Flow (FCF).
2. **Short Side (SHORT - 50% Gross / 16.67% each):** Highly concentrated positions against companies whose core value propositions were disrupted by Generative AI (`CHGG`), advertising laggers with unviable ARPU and abusive dilution (`SNAP`), and commoditized post-pandemic fad hardware with broken unit economics (`PTON`).

# LONG POSITIONS (BUY SIDE — 25% EACH)

### 1. Amazon.com, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMZN)
- **Investment Thesis:** Cloud AI re-acceleration via AWS (+28% YoY growth), regionalized logistics network driving structural retail operating margin expansion, and high-margin retail media advertising (> $50B run rate). Resilient balance sheet capable of deploying a $200B AI Capex cycle without compromising financial health.
- **Valuation:** Trades at **~28x P/E NTM**, a discount relative to historical multiples (45x-50x), offering deep asymmetric safety.

### 2. Arista Networks, Inc. (NYSE: ANET)
- **Investment Thesis:** The pure-play infrastructure winner of high-speed Ethernet switching (400G/800G/1.6T) required by massive GPU AI clusters. Consistently takes market share from legacy vendors like Cisco.
- **Financial Profile:** Revenue growth of **+35% YoY**, operating margins of **46%-47%**, zero net debt, and an asset-light model generating ROIC > 35%. Trades at **~38x P/E NTM**.

### 3. Spotify Technology S.A. (NYSE: SPOT)
- **Investment Thesis:** An operating leverage masterclass. Achieved durable pricing power ($1-$2 subscription fee increases without churn), expanding gross margins beyond 30% via its two-sided music marketplace and audiobook integration.
- **Financial Profile:** Free Cash Flow in record territory, projected EPS accelerating to ~$15.37 USD, trading at **~30x P/E NTM**.

### 4. Uber Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: UBER)
- **Investment Thesis:** Two-sided network effects spanning mobility and delivery. High EBITDA-to-FCF conversion (>70%) funding aggressive share buybacks. Serves as the indispensable software routing and demand distribution layer for autonomous vehicle (AV) fleets.
- **Valuation:** Deep value technology multiple at **~18x P/E NTM** relative to +18% top-line expansion.

# SHORT POSITIONS (CONCENTRATED SHORT SIDE — 16.67% EACH)

### 5. Chegg, Inc. (NYSE: CHGG)
- **Short Thesis:** Terminal disruption by Generative AI. Free foundation models (ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews) destroyed Chegg's paid homework subscription model.
- **Financial Reality:** Top-line revenue collapsed by **-48% YoY** (from $121M to $63M per quarter) with accelerating subscriber churn. Classic melting ice cube / value trap.

### 6. Snap Inc. (NYSE: SNAP)
- **Short Thesis:** Structural advertising share loss to Meta (Instagram Reels) and TikTok. The majority of active user growth occurs in emerging regions where average revenue per user (ARPU < $1.00 USD) fails to cover cloud server infrastructure costs.
- **Financial Reality:** Persistent negative GAAP earnings and massive stock-based compensation (SBC) dilution that transfers wealth away from public shareholders.

### 7. Peloton Interactive, Inc. (NASDAQ: PTON)
- **Short Thesis:** Post-pandemic pull-forward hangover and commoditization of connected fitness hardware. Massive fixed operating costs combined with rising subscriber churn.
- **Financial Reality:** Stressed capital structure with significant debt refinancing walls in a high-interest-rate environment, preventing sustainable positive GAAP profitability.
