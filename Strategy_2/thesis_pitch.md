## Executive Summary and Macro Thesis

Strategy #2 capitalizes on the structural divergence between two market realities:

1. **Buy Side (LONG):** Indispensable companies positioned at the **physical bottlenecks of Artificial Intelligence** (clean baseload energy and ultra-high-speed HBM memory) combined with **subscription monopolies with extreme loyalty and zero equity dilution**.

2. **Short Side (SHORT):** Post-pandemic businesses whose growth has stagnated or contracted, threatened by commoditization, **existential disruption from autonomous AI agents** and **destructive stock-based compensation (SBC) dilution** hidden behind Non-GAAP metrics.

# LONG POSITIONS (BUYS)

---

### 1. CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: CRWD)

**Central Thesis:** Cloud cybersecurity monopoly and elite unit economics.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Growth at Scale:** Sales of **$5,396.1M USD**, maintaining an acceleration rate of **+24.30% YoY**, confirming that global corporations cannot cut spending on endpoint protection.

- **Elite Software Gross Margin:** Gross margin of **75.19%** ($4,057.2M), showing that once the Falcon platform is built, each incremental dollar flows almost cleanly.

- **Real Cash Machine:** Operating Cash Flow of $2,016.6M against contained Capex of $407.9M, generating **$1,608.7M in Free Cash Flow (FCF)** (FCF margin of nearly 30%).

- **Valuation:** Trades at **55.6x EV/EBITDA**. Although the nominal valuation is demanding, it is justified by its mission-critical asset status, net retention >115%, and zero substitutability.

### 2. Costco Wholesale Corporation (NASDAQ: COST)

**Central Thesis:** The world's most resilient consumer monopoly disguised as a supermarket.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Unbreachable Moat and Scale:** Revenue of **$293,587.0M USD (+9.23% YoY)** in an inflationary environment where consumers seek to maximize purchasing power.

- **The Secret of the 12.88% Gross Margin:** Its deliberately low gross margin ($37,802.0M) is not a weakness but its greatest competitive advantage (*inverse pricing power*). The true net profit comes from **Membership** fees with renewal rates above 90%.

- **Superior Capital Efficiency (ROE 29.15%):** Turns inventory at such speed that customers pay before Costco settles with suppliers (negative working capital cycle).

- **Total Discipline Against Dilution:** Issued only **$911.0M in SBC** on a nearly $300B sales base (**barely 0.31% of revenue**), ensuring that the **$15,000.0M** in FCF reaches shareholders intact. Its NTM P/E of **42.5x** reflects its status as a top-quality safe-haven asset.

### 3. Micron Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MU)

**Central Thesis:** The HBM memory supercycle for AI at a cyclical bargain price.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Secular Growth Explosion:** Sales of **$90,274.0M USD**, registering extraordinary growth of **+166.98% YoY**. The irreplaceable demand for HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) for GPU clusters (Nvidia/AMD) transformed RAM from a commodity into a scarce strategic component.

- **Historic Profitability Expansion:** Gross margin of **72.57%** ($65,511.0M) and an **extraordinary ROE of 66.64%**.

- **Cash Flow and Balance Sheet Strength:** Generated **$51,432.0M in Operating Cash Flow** that comfortably absorbed intensive Capex of **$25,260.0M**, yielding **$26,172.0M in pure Free Cash Flow**. With total debt of only **$6,376.0M**, Micron has enough cash to settle all its liabilities in 3 months.

- **Extreme Valuation Asymmetry:** Trades at a **P/E (NTM) of only 7.1x** and **5.4x EV/EBITDA**. Wall Street applies low-cycle multiples due to fear of historical oversupply, underestimating that multi-year HBM contracts guarantee extended profitability.

### 4. Constellation Energy Corporation (NASDAQ: CEG)

**Central Thesis:** The baseload nuclear energy monopoly for AI data centers.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **The Physical Bottleneck of AI:** Server farms require continuous clean energy (24/7/365) that intermittent solar or wind sources cannot supply. As the largest nuclear operator in the U.S., it signed historic 20-year supply agreements (PPAs) with Microsoft (restarting Three Mile Island).

- **Operational Metrics:** Revenue of **$31,270.0M USD (+25.99% YoY)** with a gross margin of **22.11%**.

- **Post-Capex Cash Generation:** Despite investing **$3,911.0M in Capex** for reactor maintenance, it generated **$4,206.0M in Free Cash Flow**.

- **Capital Structure and Multiple:** Its debt of **$24,700.0M** is fully backed by regulated flows and 20-year government/corporate contracts. Its **NTM P/E of 24.2x** and **EV/EBITDA of 14.8x** reflect the revaluation of a traditional utility into a premium AI enabler.

# SHORT POSITIONS (SHORT SALES)

---

### 5. Zoom Video Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZM)

**Central Thesis:** Terminal stagnation, software commoditization, and compensatory dilution.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Inert Growth:** Revenue of **$4,933.1M USD growing at an anemic +4.98% YoY**. Zoom exhausted its corporate market by facing native and free integration from Microsoft Teams and Google Meet.

- **Massive Dilution (SBC):** Issued **$738.2M in stock-based compensation**, representing nearly **15% of the company's total revenue**. Management massively dilutes shareholders to retain talent in a no-growth company.

- **Poor Return on Capital:** Its **ROE of barely 7.97%** confirms management's inability to efficiently reinvest its excess liquidity. Its compressed multiple of **16.4x NTM P/E** is not an opportunity but a reflection of a commoditized business.

---

### 6. UiPath Inc. (NYSE: PATH)

**Central Thesis:** Existential disruption by AI agents and value trap in traditional RPA.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Imminent Technological Obsolescence:** UiPath's core business (RPA: rigid scripts that automate screen clicks and typing) is being surpassed by **Multimodal Autonomous Agents** (Claude Computer Use, OpenAI Operator, Salesforce Agentforce), which execute workflows adaptively and without code fragility.

- **Structural Deceleration:** Its growth fell from +40% post-IPO to only **+14.98% YoY ($1,720.9M)**, evidencing the brake on RPA contract renewals.

- **Cash Illusion through Dilution:** Of the reported $373.2M in FCF, **$234.6M corresponds to SBC (13.63% of revenue)**. The real cash flow adjusted for dilution is only **$138.6M**. Its PER of **14.2x NTM** represents a classic value trap.

---

### 7. DocuSign, Inc. (NASDAQ: DOCU)

**Central Thesis:** Commoditized single-product and dependence on Non-GAAP accounting adjustments.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Commoditization of E-Signature:** Revenue of **$3,361.2M growing at only +8.59% YoY**. Digital signature ceased to be a technological advantage and became a utility function absorbed by Adobe Sign and office suites.

- **Record Destructive Dilution:** Recorded **$606.2M in SBC**, consuming an alarming **18.04% of total sales**.

- **Accounting Rebound Trap:** Recent stock rebounds are due to buyback programs and Non-GAAP margins inflated by layoffs. Real GAAP EPS remains depressed and insiders have sold over $20M in shares without registering any purchases. Its **11.0x NTM P/E** multiple discounts a business in secular decline.

### 8. Teladoc Health, Inc. (NYSE: TDOC)

**Central Thesis:** Revenue contraction, continued equity destruction, and unsustainable debt.

**Key Fundamental Analysis Points:**

- **Revenue Contraction (Negative Growth):** Sales of **$2,489.5M USD**, actively contracting **(2.08)% YoY**. The post-pandemic hangover caused a return to in-person healthcare and loss of corporate clients to Amazon Clinic.

- **Chronic Losses and Capital Destruction:** The company does not generate net profits (**NTM P/E is N/A** due to continued red ink). Its **negative ROE of (12.99)%** evidences accelerated destruction of shareholder equity.

- **Debt Time Bomb:** Carries total debt of **$1,036.2M USD**. In a high-rate environment with contracting revenues, financial leverage suffocates any attempt at operational reinvention.
