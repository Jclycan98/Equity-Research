## Investment Philosophy and Selection Process

Strategy #3 implements a **"Quality Compounding"** philosophy. It does not seek to speculate on short-term price movements or bet on unproven companies, but rather to acquire concentrated stakes in the **best business models on the planet**.

### The Selection Funnel (Stock Screener):

The universe of more than 5,000 stocks listed in the U.S. was filtered under 6 non-negotiable mathematical filters:

1. **Market Capitalization:** > $10,000M (Consolidated large-cap leaders).
2. **Revenue YoY Growth:** > 12% (Businesses gaining real market share).
3. **Operating Margin:** > 20% (Defensive moats that protect against cost inflation).
4. **Return on Equity (ROE):** > 15% (Efficient capital allocation that compounds value).
5. **Free Cash Flow:** Positive and robust (Proven liquid cash generation).
6. **Net Debt / EBITDA:** < 2.0x (Armored balance sheets with no refinancing risk).

From the resulting companies, we selected a quintet that does not compete with itself and operates as **mandatory tollbooths** in five critical industries.

## 1. Seagate Technology Holdings plc (NASDAQ: STX) — Weight: 20%

### Business Model (How does it make money?)

Seagate designs, manufactures, and markets mass data storage solutions, specializing in **high-capacity mechanical hard drives (Nearline / Mass-Capacity HDDs)** for the world's largest cloud infrastructure providers (Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud).

- **The Technological Advantage (HAMR):** Its competitive moat lies in the patented **HAMR** (*Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording*) technology, which uses a microscopic laser diode to heat the disk for microseconds while writing data, enabling record storage densities of 30TB to more than 40TB per physical unit without increasing data center energy consumption.

- **Sales Channels:** Multi-year direct supply contracts with server manufacturers (OEMs) and cloud hyperscalers.

### Why did we choose it? (Strategic Reason)

1. **The Mass Storage Bottleneck in AI:** Market consensus is obsessed with compute chips (Nvidia), forgetting that language models (LLMs) and multimodal AI train on exabytes of unstructured video, audio, and text. Storing this volume on SSD / Flash memory is 5 to 7 times more expensive per terabyte. Cold and warm storage depends unavoidably on ultra-high-capacity hard drives.

2. **Consolidated Duopoly:** Seagate and Western Digital control more than 80% of the global mass HDD market, but Seagate took the technical lead in the commercial transition to HAMR.

3. **Economies of Scale and Buybacks:** Generates massive cash flows that translate into record ROE thanks to constant buying back of its own shares.

## 3. Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: HALO) — Weight: 20%

### Business Model (How does it make money?)

Halozyme operates the most profitable business model in the healthcare sector: an **Asset-Light Royalty model**.

- **The ENHANZE® Platform:** Its core is a patented enzyme (recombinant human hyaluronidase or rHuPH20) that temporarily breaks down hyaluronic acid under the skin. This allows complex biologic drugs that previously required between **3 and 6 hours of painful intravenous infusion in a hospital** to be administered in a **single 5-minute subcutaneous injection at home or in the clinic**.

- **Revenue Mechanism:** Halozyme does not finance costly phase 3 clinical trials nor maintain sales forces. It licenses the enzyme to pharmaceutical giants (Johnson & Johnson, Roche, Bristol Myers Squibb, argenx) and collects three cash flows:
  1. *Royalties:* A direct percentage (3% to 7%) of worldwide sales of approved drugs (e.g., J&J's *Darzalex Faspro* or argenx's *Vyvgart Hytrulo*).
  2. *Regulatory Milestones:* One-time multimillion-dollar cash payments each time a drug reaches an approval stage.
  3. *Product Sales:* Sale of the physical enzyme to its commercial partners.

### Why did we choose it? (Strategic Reason)

1. **The Law of Large Numbers in Its Favor:** Compared to $1 Trillion monsters like Eli Lilly (whose size makes it mathematically difficult to double its value), Halozyme has a Market Cap of only **$12B-$14B**, offering a much more agile and explosive growth runway.

2. **Zero Clinical Risk with 49% FCF Margin:** While traditional biotechs burn cash, Halozyme generates **$818.6M in FCF with only $12.4M in Capex**. J&J and Roche spend on factories and commercialization; Halozyme just cashes royalty checks.

3. **Absolute Undervaluation Anomaly (PEG 0.27x):** Trades at an **NTM P/E of only 11.0x** despite growing at **+41.15% annually**, offering an unmatched margin of safety in the market.

## 4. Moody's Corporation (NYSE: MCO) — Weight: 20%

### Business Model (How does it make money?)

Moody's is a **secular regulatory duopoly** that operates the circulatory system of global credit in two divisions:

1. **Moody's Investors Service (MIS):** The rating agency. Every time a multinational corporation, bank, or government issues debt bonds, it is obligated by regulatory mandate to hire Moody's or S&P Global to obtain a credit rating (Aaa, Baa, etc.). Without that rating, pension funds and insurers are legally prohibited from buying those bonds. Moody's charges a percentage commission on the total volume issued.

2. **Moody's Analytics (MA):** Financial software, data analytics, predictive risk models, and regulatory compliance (KYC/AML). Operates under a recurring subscription SaaS model with retention rates above 93%.

### Why did we choose it? (Strategic Reason)

1. **Inexhaustible Pricing Power:** The rating commission represents a minimal fraction of the cost of issuing debt (just a few basis points on millions of dollars), which allows Moody's to periodically raise fees without losing clients.

2. **Buying Quality at a Cyclical Discount (-26%):** We took advantage of the temporary contraction in the stock price generated by the pause that corporations took in issuing debt due to high rates. Debt issued worldwide has mandatory maturity dates (*Maturity Wall 2026-2028*), which guarantees that postponed issuances will be forcibly executed.

3. **Warren Buffett's Seal of Quality:** Berkshire Hathaway has been its largest shareholder for more than two decades due to its regulatory moat, its near-zero Capex requirement, and its 45% operating margins.

1. **The Electrical Grid Bottleneck (*The Grid*):** Artificial Intelligence data centers require gigawatts of continuous electricity that local grids cannot dispatch. GE Vernova manufactures both baseload power generation (combined-cycle gas turbines that back up renewables) and the indispensable transformers to connect a data center without collapsing the power line.

2. **Armored Sales Visibility ($176B in Backlog):** It has a record order book of **$176,000 Million dollars** (with orders growing at **+88% YoY**), equivalent to almost 4 full years of contract-guaranteed revenue.

3. **Unleveraged Balance Sheet and Post-Spinoff Cash Tsunami:** It was born free of the old financial burdens of General Electric, with only $3.7B of debt and $14.1B in FCF driven by customer advance payments on megaprojects.
